It starts calm. Then the galaxy fights back.

Tap a pod, tap another, and move the planets until every pod holds just one kind. Easy to learn, and it keeps finding new ways to surprise you.

What's inside

More than a sort game: bosses, daily puzzles and a whole wing of brain training.

Fight space bosses

Every 10 levels a boss arrives. Each of the 100 bosses has its own look and attacks: freezing pods, swapping them, hiding planets in fog, even flipping the whole board.

1000 levels across the galaxy

New planets, comets, ships and aliens keep joining the sort, with twists like frozen pods, hidden planets and supernova timers. The scenery changes every 10 levels.

Power-ups that save the day

Undo a move, ask for a hint, add an extra pod, swallow a whole planet kind with a Black Hole, or use Time Warp to jump back to where you can still win.

Something new every day

Weekly events, daily rewards and quests, the Cosmic Wheel, a Galaxy Pass with 30 reward tiers, a Planet Album and 20 pod skins.

Train your brain in The Void

50 mind-benders with rules no sort game has. Memorize a board before it goes dark, solve riddles in an exact number of moves, and flip hourglass pods.

Easy to see

Accessibility mode adds a shape symbol to every planet, so colour is never the only clue.
