19 kinds to sort: planets, comets, rockets, aliens and a sun

It starts calm. Then the galaxy fights back.

Tap a pod, tap another, and move the planets until every pod holds just one kind. Easy to learn, and it keeps finding new ways to surprise you.

Boss fights

Every 10 levels, a boss arrives

Each of the 100 bosses has its own look and its own attacks. They freeze pods, swap them around, scorch them, hide planets in fog and even flip the whole board.

  • 100 bosses, no two alike
  • Bosses rage when their health runs low
  • Beat one and win a gift of power-ups

The journey

1000 levels across the galaxy

New planets, comets, ships and aliens keep joining the sort. The scenery changes every 10 levels with a new cosmic phenomenon.

  • Twists like frozen pods, hidden planets and supernova timers
  • Chests, landmarks and galaxy gates along the path
  • Hard levels that pay double coins

In the works

A second universe

Brain training

Dare to enter The Void

50 mind-benders with rules no sort game has. Memorize a board before it goes dark, solve riddles in the exact number of moves, flip hourglass pods and tame the quantum joker planet.

  • Ghost Race: beat your own best run
